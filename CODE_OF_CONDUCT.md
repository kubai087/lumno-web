# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Lumno to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or writing in a second language.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences; critique ideas, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Lumno.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, the Discord and WeChat communities) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **i@kubai.design**. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does so, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant.
