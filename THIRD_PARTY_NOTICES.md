### Aceternity UI

Two homepage effects recreate the look of [Aceternity UI](https://ui.aceternity.com/) components. Both are written from scratch for this site, contain none of Aceternity UI's code and are covered by the MIT License:

- `src/components/ui/hero-parallax.tsx`, the tilted screenshot wall, recreates Hero Parallax: rows drifting in opposite directions on a stage that flattens as it scrolls in.
- `src/components/home/DitherShader.tsx` with `src/lib/ordered-dither.ts`, the dithered paintings, recreates the colour ordered-dither look of Dither Shader.

Earlier versions of these two files in the repository history were adapted from Aceternity UI and remain under the [Aceternity UI license](https://ui.aceternity.com/licence); don't copy them from old commits.
