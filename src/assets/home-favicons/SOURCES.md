# Bookmark favicon provenance

Stand-ins for the real site favicons the extension shows on bookmark cards and menu rows, each in its brand's official standard form. They are inlined by `SceneFavicon.astro`. Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.

- Official site artwork:
  - Material 3: `https://m3.material.io/static/assets/m3-favicon.svg` (fixed size swapped for a viewBox).
  - Material Symbols: the Google Fonts icon catalog favicon, `https://www.gstatic.com/images/icons/material/apps/fonts/1x/catalog/v5/favicon.svg`.
  - Remix Icon: `https://www.remixicon.com/img/remixicon-logo.svg`.
  - Tailwind CSS: the `tailwindcss-mark.svg` from `https://tailwindcss.com/brand`.
  - React: the atom from the react.dev header, in its light-theme brand colour `#087EA4`.
- Iconify `logos` collection (`https://api.iconify.design/logos/<name>.svg`), with the fixed width/height removed: `apple`, `google-icon`, `figma`, `nextjs-icon`, `vercel-icon`, `supabase-icon`, `github-icon`, `stackoverflow-icon`, `npm-icon`, `medium-icon`, `youtube-icon`, `dribbble-icon`, and `openai-icon`.
