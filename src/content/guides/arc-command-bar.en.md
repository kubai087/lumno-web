[Arc](https://arc.net/) shuts down on December 22, 2026. After that, Arc for Mac and Windows won't open. Before you leave, move your data out, then pick a new browser. Lumno brings several of Arc's interactions to Chrome, Dia and Edge: the command bar, switching between recent tabs, restoring pinned tabs after a restart, and automatic picture-in-picture for videos.

Lumno is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with Arc or The Browser Company.

## Arc shutdown timeline {#timeline}

- **October 22**: Arc announced the shutdown. New Arc accounts can no longer be created.
- **November 23**: Arc Search for iOS and Android loses its AI features and can no longer sign in or sync, but keeps working as a basic browser.
- **December 15**: Last day to request a copy of your Arc account data, or to ask for it to be deleted.
- **December 22**: Arc for Mac and Windows stops working. All accounts are deactivated, and account data is deleted within 30 days.

After the shutdown, you can't open Arc to reach the data stored on your computer either. For the full details, see Arc's [Arc Sunset: Key Dates and What to Expect](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44412972248471).

## Export your data before December 22 {#export}

- **Moving to Dia**: no export needed. Install Dia and choose to import from Arc. Your Spaces, pinned tabs, folders, bookmarks, history, passwords and extensions come along, and Spaces become Dia Profiles. Keep Arc installed until the import finishes. See [Move from Arc to Dia](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44413129117591).
- **Moving to another browser**: in Arc's settings, open Advanced and select Export Data. Arc saves a file named `Arc Data Export.md` that lists the links in your Favorites, pinned tabs and folders, Space by Space. It's a list of links, not a bookmarks file, so you open or bookmark the ones you want in your new browser yourself. See [How Do I Export My Data from Arc?](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/25583851606039).

Easels and Boosts can't be exported and don't come over to Dia.

## Used Lumno in Arc? Take your settings with you {#lumno-settings}

Lumno keeps its settings in the browser it's installed in, so they can't be recovered once Arc stops working. Before you switch, [export your settings](../sync/#export) in Lumno and import them after installing Lumno in your new browser. Or turn on [WebDAV sync](../sync/), which also carries your icons and wallpapers.

Even if Dia brings the extension over, export a copy first, just in case.

## Choose your next browser {#choose}

| Browser | What it shares with Arc | With Lumno |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dia | Made by the same team, with a sidebar, pinned tabs and a tab switcher; imports straight from Arc | Command bar works; new tab not yet available |
| Chrome, Edge | No sidebar or Spaces, but the widest choice of extensions | Everything works |
| [Zen Browser](https://zen-browser.app/) | Firefox-based, with a sidebar, workspaces and split view, much like Arc | Install from Firefox Add-ons; a few features are limited |
| [Vivaldi](https://vivaldi.com/) | Sidebar, workspaces and split view | Everything works |

For details on each browser, see [Browser compatibility](../browser-compatibility/).

## Arc and Lumno compared {#mapping}

| Arc | Lumno |
| --- | --- |
| `⌘ T` opens the command bar | `⌘ ⇧ K` opens the command bar; you can change it to `⌘ T` |
| Switch to an open tab from the command bar | Select a result marked "Opened" and press `↩` |
| `⌃ Tab` switches between recent tabs | `⌥ Q` opens the recent tab switcher |
| `⌘ ⇧ C` copies the URL | Assign a shortcut to "Copy Current Page URL" on the extensions page |
| Pinned tabs come back after a restart | Turn on "Restore Pinned Tabs After Restart" in Lumno's settings |
| Videos move into picture-in-picture when you switch tabs | [Auto picture-in-picture](../auto-pip/), on by default |
| New tab | Lumno's new tab (not yet available in Dia) |

## How to switch {#steps}

1. [Install Lumno](../install/): from the Chrome Web Store for Chrome and Dia, or from Edge Add-ons for Edge.
2. Open `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` and set "Open command bar" to `⌘ T`, the same key as in Arc and [Zen](https://zen-browser.app/). See [Keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts/#change). In Dia, free `⌘ T` first (see below).
3. Press `⌘ T` and try [searching your tabs, bookmarks and history](../search-tabs-bookmarks-history/).
4. To move between your last few pages, press `⌥ Q` to open the [recent tab switcher](../tab-switcher/).

Dia's own shortcuts take priority, so in Dia, first open Settings → Shortcuts and change "New Tab" from `⌘ T` to another key. See [Browser compatibility](../browser-compatibility/#dia). If the shortcut doesn't work in Dia, change its scope from "In Dia" to "Global" on the extensions page.

## Arc features Lumno can't provide {#limits}

A sidebar, Spaces and split view depend on the browser itself, so an extension can't add them. If you rely on them, choose Dia, Zen or Vivaldi from the table above. For more, see [What Lumno can't do](../limitations/).
