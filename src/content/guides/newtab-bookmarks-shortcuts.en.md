Your go-to sites sit under the search box, one click away. And however many bookmarks you have, there's no digging.

## Shortcuts {#shortcuts}

The row of icons under the search box are your shortcuts. Hover over them and they magnify like the macOS Dock.

![A row of shortcut icons under the search box; the Figma icon under the pointer is magnified with its name shown](nt-shortcuts "Your go-to sites, one click away")

### Add one

1. Click the "+" at the end of your shortcuts.
2. Enter a name and URL. Browser pages like `chrome://inspect/#devices` work too.
3. Choose an icon: built-in, online, or upload your own (a square image of 128×128 px or larger works best).
4. Save.

### Tidy up

- Right-click a shortcut to edit or remove it. Removed the wrong one? The notice shows a shortcut to undo.
- Drag to reorder; drag one onto your bookmarks to turn it into a bookmark.
- Shortcuts per row, icon size, spacing, the Dock magnification and the "+" button are all in Lumno's settings under Appearance → Shortcuts.

## Bookmarks {#bookmarks}

The bookmarks area shows what's in your browser's bookmarks bar. Hover over a folder to open it.

![The new tab's bookmarks area: folders such as Reference, Design guidelines and Competitors, plus ChatGPT and GitHub bookmarks](nt-bookmarks-folder "Multi-layer folder view")

### Three layouts

Click "···" at the top right of the bookmarks area to switch:

- Multi-layer folder view: folders as cards that open level by level on hover.
- Multi-level list view: browse folder contents as nested menus.
- Top bookmarks bar: bookmarks in a row along the top of the page, like the browser's own bar.

![The top bookmarks bar layout, with bookmark folders in a row along the top of the new tab](nt-bookmarks-top "Top bookmarks bar")

With the top bookmarks bar you can also change its background: adaptive mist, clear glass, transparent, or a color of your own.

### A few more details

- Lots of bookmarks? Page through them with the arrows at the top right, or change how many rows show in settings.
- Right-click a folder to change its color; the same folder takes that color in menus too.
- Click the bookmark icon to open the browser's bookmark manager.
