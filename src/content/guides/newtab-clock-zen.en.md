The time, right where you look. And when you need to focus, nothing but the search box.

## Clock {#clock}

1. Click the Appearance button in the bottom-right corner of the new tab.
2. In the Search box section, set "Content above the search bar" to Time.

You can change the time's font weight and show seconds. Choose Brand to show the Lumno wordmark, or Hide to show nothing.

![The new tab with the clock 09:41 above the search box and shortcuts below](nt-clock "A clock above the search box")

## Zen mode {#zen}

Type `/zen` in the new tab's search box and press Return. Everything but the search box and wallpaper tucks away. Type `/zen` again to bring it all back.

![The new tab in Zen mode: only the search box over a Monet coastline](nt-zen "Just the search box, when you need to focus")

Zen mode syncs with your browser, so your other computers stay quiet too.
