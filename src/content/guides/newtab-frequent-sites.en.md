The bottom of the new tab shows the sites you visit most. You can pin the ones you use every day, and for shows and serials, a card can keep track of where you left off.

## Frequent sites {#frequent}

By default, the cards at the bottom show your most visited sites. Click a card to open the site.

![Most visited cards at the bottom of the new tab: GitHub, Material, Figma and Mozilla](nt-recent "Most visited")

To see recently visited sites instead, click "···" at the top right of the cards and choose "Show Recent". In Lumno's settings, under New Tab Bottom Content, you can show one row, two rows or none.

## Pin a site {#pin}

Hover over a card and click the pin icon at its bottom right. You can pin up to three cards. Click the pin again to unpin a card. Pinned cards sync through your browser.

## Track progress in a show or serial {#progress}

When you pin a show or a serialized novel, Lumno starts tracking your progress. When you reach the next episode or chapter of the same title, the card updates to it, so next time you can continue from where you left off.

- A card that's tracking progress shows a radar icon. Click the icon to stop tracking.
- If you don't need this feature, turn off "Keep pinned shows and novels up to date" in Lumno's settings.

Progress is updated on your computer based on the pages you open, and is never sent anywhere.
