At the bottom of the new tab are the places you go most. Pin the ones you use every day; for shows and serials, the card keeps your place for you.

## Your frequent sites {#frequent}

The cards at the bottom show your most visited sites by default. Click one to go.

![Most visited cards at the bottom of the new tab: GitHub, Material, Figma and Mozilla](nt-recent "Most visited")

To see where you've been lately instead, click "···" at the top right of the cards and choose recent. In Lumno's settings, under New Tab Bottom Content, show one row, two rows, or none.

## Pin the ones you use {#pin}

Hover over a card and click the pin at its bottom right. You can pin up to three; click again to unpin. Pinned cards sync with your browser.

## Keep your place in a show {#progress}

Pin a show or a serialized novel and Lumno starts following your progress: when you reach the next episode or chapter of the same title, the card switches to it, so next time you pick up right where you left off.

- A card that's tracking shows a radar icon. Click it to stop.
- Don't need it? Turn off "Keep pinned shows and novels up to date" in Lumno's settings.

Progress is updated on your computer from the pages you open, and isn't sent anywhere.
