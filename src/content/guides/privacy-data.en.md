Lumno has no accounts and no servers that store user data. Your tabs, bookmarks and history are handled only in your own browser and are never sent to us. This is a short summary; for the full text, see the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/).

## What stays on your device {#local}

To search, switch and show the new tab, Lumno reads these on your device:

- Your open tabs, history, bookmarks and top sites
- What you type in Lumno and the results you pick, so the ones you use rise to the top
- Your settings, and any icons and wallpapers you upload

The switcher's page previews stay on your device too: at most 12, each kept for up to two hours.

## What we don't collect {#not-collected}

No sign-up, no login. Lumno doesn't collect your name or email, and doesn't report usage counts, clicks, device identifiers or crash data.

## Requests that leave your computer {#requests}

These go straight from the extension to the service involved, never through us:

| Request | Sent to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search suggestions | Your search engine (such as Google or Bing) | As you type in Lumno's search box |
| Searches and AI questions | The site or AI you chose | When you press Return |
| Site icons | Google's favicon service or Favicon.is | When showing site icons |
| Wallpapers | Bing, Lorem Picsum, the Cleveland Museum of Art | When you use online wallpapers |
| Update notes | GitHub | After the extension updates |

## How settings sync {#sync}

- With your browser: theme, shortcuts, wallpaper choice and similar settings sync through your browser account.
- WebDAV (optional): off by default. Once on, your full settings, uploaded icons and wallpapers are stored on a server you choose. History, bookmarks and tabs are not uploaded.
- Export and import: save your settings to a file and import it on another computer.

For how to set it up, see [Sync](../sync/).

## About this website {#website}

This website (lumno.kubai.design) uses analytics to see how many people visit and where they come from. That's separate from the extension, which loads no analytics at all.
