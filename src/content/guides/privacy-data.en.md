Lumno has no accounts and no servers that store user data. Your tabs, bookmarks and history are processed only in your own browser and are never sent to us. This page is a summary; for the full text, see the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/).

## Data stored on your device {#local}

To provide search, tab switching and the new tab, Lumno reads and stores the following on your device:

- Your open tabs, history, bookmarks and top sites
- What you type in Lumno and the results you choose, so that results you use often appear higher
- Your settings, and any icons and wallpapers you upload

The switcher's page previews are also stored only on your device: up to 12, each kept for up to two hours.

## Information we don't collect {#not-collected}

There's no sign-up and no login. Lumno doesn't collect your name or email address, and doesn't report usage counts, clicks, device identifiers or crash data.

## Requests sent outside your device {#requests}

These requests go directly from the extension to the service involved, never through us:

| Request | Sent to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search suggestions | Your search engine (such as Google or Bing) | As you type in Lumno's search box |
| Searches and AI questions | The site or AI you choose | When you press Return |
| Site icons | Google's favicon service or Favicon.is | When site icons are shown |
| Wallpapers | Bing, Lorem Picsum, the Cleveland Museum of Art | When you use online wallpapers |
| Update notes | GitHub | After the extension updates |

## How settings sync {#sync}

- Browser sync: your theme, shortcuts, wallpaper choice and similar settings sync through your browser account.
- WebDAV sync (optional): off by default. When it's on, your full settings, uploaded icons and wallpapers are stored on a server you choose. History, bookmarks and tabs are not uploaded.
- Export and import: save your settings to a file and import it on another computer.

For setup instructions, see [Sync](../sync/).

## About this website {#website}

This website (lumno.kubai.design) uses analytics to measure how many people visit and where they come from. This is separate from the extension, which doesn't load any analytics.
