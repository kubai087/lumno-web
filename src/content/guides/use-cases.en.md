Everyone opens Lumno for a slightly different reason. See which of these sounds like you.

## Dozens of tabs open {#many-tabs}

Finding a page takes longer than opening it? Press `⌘ ⇧ K`, type a few letters from the title, and press Return on the result marked "Opened". You land on that tab instead of opening a duplicate.

- To search only your open tabs, press `Tab` once while the box is empty.
- To bounce between the last two or three pages, press `⌥ Q`.

If tabs are slowing your computer down, pair Lumno with Chrome's own Memory Saver, or tuck tabs into a list with [OneTab](https://www.one-tab.com/).

See [Search tabs, bookmarks and history](../search-tabs-bookmarks-history/) and [Switch between recent tabs](../tab-switcher/).

## Just left Arc {#from-arc}

Since Arc stopped getting new features, plenty of people have moved to Dia, Chrome or Edge, and the command bar is what they miss most. Install Lumno, set the shortcut to `⌘ T`, and it feels familiar again.

See [Moving from Arc](../arc-command-bar/).

## Living in docs and GitHub {#developers}

- `gh`, `mdn`, `npm` or `sf`, then `Tab`, searches GitHub, MDN, npm or Stack Overflow directly.
- Type an address like `chrome://extensions` to open it.
- Add your company's docs or issue tracker as a custom search with its own shortcut.

To browse the web from the keyboard too, pair it with [Vimium](https://vimium.github.io/).

See [Site search and asking AI](../site-search/).

## Research and writing {#research}

That article from last week, that report you bookmarked: search your bookmarks and history together. When a passage is hard going, select it and ask AI to explain, translate or summarize.

## Asking AI first {#ai}

Type `gpt`, `gm` or `pplx`, press `Tab`, write your question and hit Return. No need to open the AI's site first.

See [Site search and asking AI](../site-search/).

## A calm new tab {#quiet-new-tab}

Pick a painting you love, or a new Bing photo every day. Put a clock above the search box. When you need to focus, type `/zen` and keep only the search box.

See [Change the wallpaper](../newtab-wallpaper/) and [Clock and Zen mode](../newtab-clock-zen/).

## Following a show or a serial {#series}

Pin the show or web novel you're following to your new tab. When you reach the next episode or chapter, the card updates itself, so next time you pick up right where you left off.

See [Frequent sites and progress cards](../newtab-frequent-sites/).
