Lumno is a free, open-source browser extension that does two things. Press a shortcut on any page and a command bar appears that can find anything in your browser. And your new tab becomes a calm, good-looking place to start. It works in Chrome, Edge, Dia, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

## The command bar {#command-bar}

Press `⌘ ⇧ K` (`Ctrl + Shift + K` on Windows) on any page and the command bar opens in the middle of the screen.

- Find an open tab, a bookmark or a page from your history with a few letters, then press Return to jump straight to it. See [Search tabs, bookmarks and history](../search-tabs-bookmarks-history/).
- Search inside GitHub, YouTube or Stack Overflow without visiting their home page first: type a shortcut, press `Tab`, search. Asking ChatGPT or Gemini works the same way. See [Site search and asking AI](../site-search/).
- Type a URL, or a browser page like `chrome://settings`, and open it.

![Lumno's command bar on a blank page: typing chrome lists an open GitHub tab, search suggestions, a bookmark and top sites](search "One box for everything in your browser")

To bounce between the last few pages you used, press `⌥ Q` for the [recent tab switcher](../tab-switcher/).

## The new tab {#new-tab}

Once Lumno is installed, the new tab in Chrome and Edge becomes Lumno's: a painting for a wallpaper, your go-to sites, your bookmarks and the places you visit most, all on one screen. Keep it minimal or fill it up in Appearance settings.

![Lumno's new tab: a clock and search box at the top, shortcuts and bookmark folders in the middle, most visited sites below, over a Monet coastline](newtab "Lumno's new tab")

## Small things that add up {#more}

- Select text, ask AI: highlight a passage and have AI answer, translate, explain or summarize it. Off by default.
- Web clip: cut out part of a page into a floating picture-in-picture window.
- Auto picture-in-picture: leave a YouTube or Twitch tab and the video keeps playing in a small window.
- Pinned tabs come back after you restart the browser.

Each one has its own switch in Lumno's settings. Keep only what you use.

## Why Lumno {#strengths}

- Free and open source: GPL-3.0, with all the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/kubai087/lumno-extension).
- No account: Lumno has no sign-up and no servers. Your browsing data stays in your browser. See [Privacy and data](../privacy-data/).
- No browser switch: get an Arc-style command bar in Chrome, Edge or Dia.
- Speaks your language: the interface comes in English, Japanese and Simplified and Traditional Chinese, with sites from around the world built in.

Before installing, it's worth a look at [what Lumno can't do](../limitations/).
