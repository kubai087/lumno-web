Lumno is a free, open-source browser extension with two main parts. Press a shortcut on any page to open a command bar that searches your whole browser, and get a clean, good-looking start page on every new tab. It works in Chrome, Edge, Dia, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

## The command bar {#command-bar}

Press `⌘ ⇧ K` (`Ctrl + Shift + K` on Windows) on any page, and the command bar opens in the middle of the screen.

- Type a few letters to find an open tab, a bookmark or a page from your history, then press Return to go straight to it. See [Search tabs, bookmarks and history](../search-tabs-bookmarks-history/).
- Search GitHub, YouTube or Stack Overflow without opening their home page first: type the site's shortcut, press `Tab`, then type your search. Asking ChatGPT or Gemini works the same way. See [Site search and asking AI](../site-search/).
- Type a URL, or a browser page such as `chrome://settings`, to open it directly.

![Lumno's command bar on a blank page: typing chrome lists an open GitHub tab, search suggestions, a bookmark and top sites](search "One search box for your whole browser")

To move between the last few pages you used, press `⌥ Q` to open the [recent tab switcher](../tab-switcher/).

## The new tab {#new-tab}

After you install Lumno, the new tab in Chrome and Edge becomes Lumno's start page: a painting as the wallpaper, your favorite sites, your bookmarks and the places you visit most, all on one screen. You can make it as minimal or as full as you like in Appearance settings.

![Lumno's new tab: a clock and search box at the top, shortcuts and bookmark folders in the middle, most visited sites below, over an impressionist orchard](newtab "Lumno's new tab")

## More features {#more}

- [Selection quick actions](../selection-actions/): select a passage and have AI answer, translate, explain or summarize it. The button appears only when Lumno judges the text likely to need help. Off by default; turn it on under Labs in settings.
- [Web clip](../web-clip/): clip part of a page into a floating picture-in-picture window.
- [Auto picture-in-picture](../auto-pip/): when you leave a YouTube or Twitch tab, the video keeps playing in a small window.
- Restore pinned tabs: with "Restore Pinned Tabs After Restart" turned on, pinned tabs come back automatically after you restart the browser.

You can turn each of these on or off in Lumno's settings.

## Why Lumno {#strengths}

- Free and open source: licensed under GPL-3.0, with all the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/kubai087/lumno-extension).
- No account needed: Lumno has no sign-up and no servers, and your browsing data stays in your browser. See [Privacy and data](../privacy-data/).
- No need to switch browsers: get an Arc-style command bar in Chrome, Edge or Dia.
- Multilingual: the interface is available in English, Japanese, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese, with popular sites from around the world built in.

Before you install, you may also want to read [what Lumno can't do](../limitations/).
