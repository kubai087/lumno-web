## Summary

Lumno has no accounts and no servers that store user data. Your tabs, bookmarks and history are processed only in your own browser and are never sent to us.

- There's no sign-up and no login. Lumno doesn't collect your name or email address, and doesn't report usage counts, clicks, device identifiers or crash data.
- Your theme, shortcuts, wallpaper choice and similar settings sync through your browser account. WebDAV sync is off by default; when it's on, your full settings, uploaded icons and wallpapers are stored on a server you choose. History, bookmarks and tabs are not uploaded.
- This website uses analytics to measure how many people visit and where they come from. This is separate from the extension, which doesn't load any analytics.

These requests go directly from the extension to the service involved, never through us:

| Request | Sent to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search suggestions | Your search engine (such as Google or Bing) | As you type in Lumno's search box |
| Searches and AI questions | The site or AI you choose | When you press Return |
| Site icons | Google's favicon service or Favicon.is | When site icons are shown |
| Wallpapers | Bing, Lorem Picsum, the Cleveland Museum of Art | When you use online wallpapers |
| Update notes | GitHub | After the extension updates |
| Help Center search | The Lumno website (GitHub Pages) | When you search the Lumno Help Center; only the index is downloaded, not what you type |

This summary is for convenience; the full policy below governs.
